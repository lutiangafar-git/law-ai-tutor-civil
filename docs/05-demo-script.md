# 2-Minute Demo Script: Law AI Tutor

**Before recording**
- Use a **Chrome Incognito** window (proves the live URL works for anyone, with no saved data).
- Open https://law-tutor-drill.vercel.app. Close other tabs and turn notifications off.
- Record with **Windows + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar) or Loom (loom.com, free). Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** or keep the Loom link, and check that it opens in Incognito.
- Have this answer ready to paste (it is deliberately weak):
  `Answer: B1 has the better right. Legal basis: Art. 1544 on double sale. Application: B1 bought first and paid in full, B2 only pays in installments. Conclusion: B1 owns the land.`

| Time | Show on screen | Say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Home of the live site | "This is Law AI Tutor. Philippine law schools are restricting AI because students use it as a shortcut, and over half of bar examinees are repeaters who don't know why their answers fail. This tutor makes you answer first." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Onboarding: the Year 1–4 and bar choices → pick **Year 2 student** → usage notice | "You choose your year. Year 1 and 2 students can't use it during class hours, and it never helps with graded work. Each year sees the Civil Law subject it's taking: Year 2 sees Sales." |
| 0:30–0:40 | Home: the Sales topic and the amber **Demo** banner | "Every topic comes from a source file. Until a lawyer signs it off, the app says so openly." |
| 0:40–1:15 | **Problem drill** → paste the weak answer → **Submit** → show the ALAC scores and the follow-up question | "This is a real 2010 bar question. I'll submit a weak answer. Claude scores it on Answer, Legal basis, Application and Conclusion. It's a Practice score, not official bar grading. It doesn't give me the answer; it asks a targeted question about the agent's authority, and I have to retry before I see the model answer." |
| 1:15–1:35 | Settings → switch to **Bar reviewee (repeater)** → home shows the diagnostic and all subjects → open a **Recitation drill** → type *"What did the Supreme Court rule in Nuguid v. Nuguid? Give the G.R. number."* → Send | "A bar repeater sees all of Civil Law and gets a diagnostic first. And if I ask about a case that's not in the reviewed sources, it refuses to invent a citation and steers me back to the provision." |
| 1:35–1:50 | GitHub repo: README → **Actions** tab (green ✓) | "The code is public on GitHub. Every push runs lint, 48 tests, a production build and a secret scan, then Vercel deploys automatically. The API key is only in Vercel's environment settings." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Back to the app | "Next: lawyer sign-off of the topics, and a paid pilot with bar reviewees. Thank you." |

**Tip:** if the AI takes a few seconds to reply, keep talking about what it's checking. Do one practice run first.
