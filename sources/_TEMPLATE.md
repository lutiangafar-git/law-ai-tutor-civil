# Source text
<!-- Paste the exact provision text from the official source. The reviewer confirms it word for word. -->
Art. 1156. An obligation is a juridical necessity to give, to do or not to do.

# Key points for the tutor (reviewer-approved)
<!-- Short, plain statements the tutor may use in feedback. Nothing else counts as legal content.
     These also power the Year 3–4 In-class Quick Check. -->
- An obligation is a juridical (legally enforceable) necessity.
- Its object is a prestation: to give, to do, or not to do.
